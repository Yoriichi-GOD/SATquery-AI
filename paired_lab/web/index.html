Input requirements

Spectral comparison: two Sentinel-2 surface-reflectance TIFFs with labelled Red/Green/NIR and genuine SCL bands, matching projected grids and earlier/later acquisition dates. Ask "Analyze these images" for both indices, or name vegetation/NDVI or water/NDWI. Built-up expansion is unavailable. Calibration scales/offsets must match the actual data.

Temporal: two corresponding 256 × 256 uint8 RGB GeoTIFFs, with red/green/blue band descriptions and ordered dates.

Optical–SAR: a 120 × 120, 10 m projected pair. Sentinel-1 VV/VH in dB; Sentinel-2 B02/B03/B04/B05/B06/B07/B08/B8A/B11/B12 in reflectance × 10,000. Label each band and include sensor and units TIFF tags.

Water mapping: 512 × 512 Sentinel-1 GRD VV/VH + 13-band Sentinel-2 L1C, labelled bands, sensor/units/acquisition_date tags. The supplied multi-country samples include these details.

Uploaded grids and CRS must match. Temporal/classification crops must be fully valid; water excludes pixels without common finite inputs. Sample pairs use the publisher’s correspondence manifest; their TIFF exports have no invented map coordinates.
