# SATQuery versus public SIH 2026 / PS26167 repositories

Audit date: **7 November 2026**. Source inspection and retained-artifact comparison; competitor runtimes were not executed.

**Snapshot boundary:** this audit was completed before the subsequent README/research publication work. Its public-main pin is the source observed during that audit, not a permanent assertion about the latest branch. Later commits do not change the recorded comparison inputs.

## Verdict

**Our current local system is substantially further along than the inspected scaffold and planning-only snapshots. It is also a serious competitor to the more complete implementations in workflow breadth, geospatial evidence handling and case-study research. An overall claim that we are “far ahead” of the SIH field, or more accurate than its strongest models, is not established.**

The strongest competitors are doing real work: adapted VLM distribution, controlled building-change training, broad VQA benchmark reporting, bounded execution with immutable artifacts, and confidence-calibration analysis. Their narrower strengths cannot be dismissed by counting our features or software tests.

At audit time, our most immediate disadvantage was publication: **the newest local terrain and validation work was absent from public main at the inspected pin**. Reviewers of that snapshot could not verify the entire local release. The comparison audit itself did not push, merge, execute competitors, or change product functionality. Subsequent publication is a separate operation.

## Scope and comparability

- GitHub searches for `"satquery" "SIH"` returned **93 distinct keyword-matched repository candidates** across two pages. Broader SatQuery, PS26167 and satellite/SIH2026 searches supplemented discovery. These are not 93 verified teams, finalists or final submissions; companion repositories, forks and unrelated matches can exist.
- **Ten competitor repositories received deeper source/artifact inspection**, selected for relevance, substantial implementations and different engineering approaches. This is a purposive sample, not a census or a numerical league table. The candidate list and full source pins are in [DISCOVERY_AND_PINS.json](DISCOVERY_AND_PINS.json).
- We inspected code paths, configurations, checkpoint metadata, evaluation artifacts and public status where accessible. We did not run competitor code/tests/models, download weights, test hosted demos, inspect private systems or obtain organizer submission records. Model quality remains author-reported unless explicitly identified as our own retained execution evidence.
- Inactivity is not a verified freeze. All ten inspected repositories were unarchived when checked. `awdtyo` has an earlier v0.1.0 tag and `siddhtyagi18` has an earlier demo-freeze report; both current heads postdate those artifacts. No official final-submission freeze was established. Names such as `final` do not prove one. See the linked detailed notes for metadata sources.
- PS26167-style comparison here concerns adaptation, single-image VQA/grounding, temporal reasoning, optical–SAR analysis, automatic routing, input checks, visual evidence, confidence and auditable outputs. Our additional terrain/exposure research is a useful distinction, not a substitute for these core tasks.

## Where the stronger peers expose our gaps

1. **Public restoration and release visibility.** Awdtyo publishes inspectable adapter files; our local LoRA/CDVQA models still require manual releases. The fresh CPU restoration verified only its documented subset. Our newest research and component integration are not in public main. [Our asset contract](../../../docs/ASSETS.md), [our restoration record](../../../docs/REPRODUCIBILITY_VERIFICATION.md), [adapter audit](ADAPTER_TEAMS_AUDIT.md).
2. **Task breadth is not a matched benchmark suite.** Cattolatte publishes VRSBench/RSVQA/grounding results we cannot presently match with an equivalent current, reproducible run. Siddhtyagi has a clearer train→baseline→experiment record for one narrow change task. Our historical MCI result uses an inherited checkpoint. [Their artifacts](AGENT_TEAMS_AUDIT.md), [change experiments](ADAPTER_TEAMS_AUDIT.md), [our claim register](../../../docs/CLAIM_REGISTER.md).
3. **Calibrated uncertainty remains incomplete.** STARFORGE exposes calibration analysis; several other repos use uncalibrated heuristics. Our quality flags, abstentions and mask sensitivity are useful but do not establish calibrated correctness probabilities. Compare calibration on a fixed independent support rather than inventing a universal confidence percentage. [STARFORGE evidence](OTHER_TEAMS_AUDIT.md), [our remaining gates](../validation-closure-20261107/completion.json).
4. **Terrain benefit and exposure transfer remain research hypotheses.** Independent case-specific exposure labels, trustworthy sensor registration and suitable drainage/terrain remain incomplete. Some terrain additions worsened measured performance. No validated Cartosat/RISAT comparison or broad novelty claim is closed. [Current completion gates](../validation-closure-20261107/completion.json), [case diagnostic](../case-campaign-20261107/REPORT.md).
5. **Automatic routing is not an autonomous research agent.** Our investigation is a deterministic supported-tool workflow. Most inspected comparators are deterministic too; a LangGraph diagram or external LLM call alone also does not establish an adaptive, verified scientific agent. Describe the actual execution policy. [Our investigation](../../../jai_extension/investigation.py), [peer source audit](AGENT_TEAMS_AUDIT.md).

## Decisions justified by this audit

The bottleneck is closing and exposing existing work, not adding another specialist. These are proposed follow-up actions; this audit performed no additional implementation or publication:

1. **Prepare one reviewer-visible release** that distinguishes the public and local snapshots, preserves Day 1 and every frozen research pack, restores required assets, and demonstrates the existing Nepal flow. Release only material with established distribution rights; local raw datasets are not automatically publishable.
2. **Freeze a common evaluation protocol before rerunning anyone.** Select a small set of strong peers for comparable tasks, exact data/splits/preprocessing/checkpoint identities, prediction logs and uncertainty intervals. Keep CDVQA classifiers, generative VQA, land cover, change masks and exposure as separate experiments. Include question-only/image-shuffle controls and acquisition-inclusive versus inference-only latency boundaries where measured.
3. **Close the exposure prerequisites.** Obtain case-specific independent labels and registration/terrain evidence, then compare proximity, terrain and full graph on the same withheld support. Retain negative results. Do not consume the reserve to tune a nicer headline.
4. **Test evaluation-to-product parity.** Submit ordinary app requests and check the effective model, parameters, masks and provenance against the benchmark path. The peer audit found real benchmark/serving mismatches; our release needs to demonstrate parity rather than assume it.

What we can truthfully say now: **SATQuery has a broad, evidence-preserving local geospatial investigation implementation and concrete multi-case research measurements, beyond several inspected public prototypes. Its overall accuracy advantage, validated exposure transfer and field-wide novelty are still unproved.**
