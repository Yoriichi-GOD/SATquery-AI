# 90-second flood-impact demo script

**Target duration:** 1:30 maximum. Read only the quoted lines aloud; click cues are silent. Before recording, open the final report PDF in a second window at page 5. Start at Workspace with event-fe47db331390 selected and the page at the top.

| Time | What to click while speaking | Say exactly |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:08 | Click **Workspace**. In **Saved analysis**, select event-fe47db331390 if needed. | “This is SatQuery's separate flood workspace. I'm opening the saved Syapru run for 26 August.” |
| 0:08-0:21 | Open **View**; select **Before · optical RGB**; click the image to open zoom, use the wheel, press **Esc**, then select **After · optical RGB**. | “These are the available optical dates. The water calculation compares Sentinel-1 scenes from 16 and 28 August on orbit 85.” |
| 0:21-0:35 | Open **View** and choose **Impact layers**. Point to the candidate and exposure metrics. | “This run marks 95,600 square metres of water candidates, two intersected buildings, 185.85 metres of roads, and two bridge features.” |
| 0:35-0:44 | Point to the question box and click **Ask** with the prefilled water-area question. | “These indicate possible exposure, not confirmed damage. Answers use this run's saved calculations.” |
| 0:44-0:56 | Scroll to **Processing record** and point to its seven steps; expand **Settlement access**. | “The processing record lists seven steps. Access remains unknown because no hub was snapped; unknown is not proof of safety.” |
| 0:56-1:08 | Click **Evidence**. Point at the regression-check table and source/download links. | “CDSE imagery and HeiGIT plus OpenStreetMap road history feed one traceable workflow. Seventy software tests passed.” |
| 1:08-1:20 | Press Alt+Tab to the report PDF, open on page 5; point at the UNOSAT results. | “Five full area/date runs averaged 51.6 seconds. UNOSAT agreement was poor: IoU 0.013, recall 1.37%, against a preliminary mixed extent.” |
| 1:20-1:30 | Keep report page 5 visible. | “Debris and real access remain unvalidated. This may help analysts prioritize review; costs and time savings are unmeasured.” |

The script is about 142 spoken words. Rehearse once with a timer. If navigating the optical views takes too long, leave Impact layers selected and say the optical dates while pointing to the View menu. Do not imply the 51.6-second, five-run sample is a service-wide average, the reference score is flood accuracy, or an unknown road-access result is a confirmed closure.

**Sources used in the report:** CDSE Sentinel data; HeiGIT ohsome and OpenStreetMap historical roads; Copernicus DEM; EMSR927 and UNOSAT Product 4259 for checking only. The report lists exact URLs, licenses/quotas, test results, run measurements, cost limits, and evaluation methods.
